Types of Cyber Fraud

🔐 Phishing

Phishing is a type of cyber attack where attackers send fake emails or messages to trick users into sharing sensitive information like passwords or bank details.

Prevention: Always check the sender and avoid clicking unknown links.

📱 OTP Fraud

In OTP fraud, scammers call or message users pretending to be bank officials and ask for OTP to access their accounts.

Prevention: Never share your OTP with anyone.

💸 UPI Scam

Fraudsters send fake payment requests or links to trick users into transferring money.

Prevention: Always verify before approving any payment request.

⚠️ Job Scam

Fake job offers asking for registration fees or personal information.

Prevention: Never pay money for job applications.
