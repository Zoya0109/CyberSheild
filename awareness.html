Test Your Awareness

You receive a call asking for OTP to “verify your bank account”. What will you do?

Never share your OTP with anyone. Banks never ask for OTP. This is a fraud attempt.

You receive a message: "You won a lottery! Click this link." What will you do?

This is a scam. Do not click unknown links.

Someone sends you a QR code and asks you to scan it to receive money. Is it safe?

No. Scanning unknown QR codes can lead to unauthorized money deduction.

A stranger on social media is asking for personal photos and threatening you. What should you do?

Do not respond. Block the user and report to cyber crime portal immediately.

You receive a message offering a high-paying job with upfront registration fee. Is it real?

No. Genuine companies never ask for money to offer jobs.

You get an email saying “Your account will be blocked, click link immediately”. What should you do?

Do not click links. Verify directly from official website or app.

You find a phone number on Google claiming to be bank customer care. What risk is there?

Scammers often post fake numbers to steal personal data.
